# Curriculum Overview

Eighteen skills currently have thirty-six active parameterized templates: six fraction, six algebra and six ratio/percent skills, each with two distinct v2.0.0 templates. Every implemented skill has two verified examples, prerequisite/remediation metadata, standards references, misconception descriptions and deterministic operand figures. Three difficulty ranges and preserved seeds/parameters make generation reproducible. Saved v1 question snapshots remain unchanged and the legacy generator remains available.

The target of 24–32 skills and at least 48 templates is not yet reached. Geometry, arbitrary unit-aware rules and labeled first-error precision evaluation remain open. See [[Fractions]], [[Question-Generation]] and [[Educational-Standards]].
