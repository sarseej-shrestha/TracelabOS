# Curriculum Overview

Twenty-four skills currently have forty-eight active parameterized templates: six skills each in fractions, algebra, ratios/percentages and geometry, each with two distinct v2.0.0 templates. Every implemented skill has two verified examples, prerequisite/remediation metadata, standards references, misconception descriptions and deterministic operand figures. Three difficulty ranges and preserved seeds/parameters make generation reproducible. Saved v1 question snapshots remain unchanged and the legacy generator remains available.

The initial numerical curriculum target of 24 skills and 48 templates is reached. This is scoped coverage, not comprehensive standards coverage: arbitrary symbolic/unit-aware rules and labeled first-error precision evaluation remain open.
