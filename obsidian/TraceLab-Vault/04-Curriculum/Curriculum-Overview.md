# Curriculum Overview

Eight skills currently have fourteen active parameterized templates: six fraction skills with two templates each (v2.0.0), plus two original algebra skills/templates. Every fraction skill has two verified examples, prerequisite/remediation metadata, standards references, misconception descriptions and deterministic operand figures. Three difficulty ranges and preserved seeds/parameters make generation reproducible. Saved v1 question snapshots remain unchanged and the legacy generator remains available.

The target of 24–32 skills and at least 48 templates is not yet reached. Ratios, geometry, broader algebra, unit-aware rules and labeled first-error precision evaluation remain open. See [[Fractions]], [[Question-Generation]] and [[Educational-Standards]].
