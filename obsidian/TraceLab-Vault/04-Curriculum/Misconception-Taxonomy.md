# Misconception Taxonomy

DISTRIBUTE_ALL_TERMS identifies a*(b±c) transformed to a*b±c by comparing the malformed expansion. ADD_DENOMINATORS identifies (a+c)/(b+d) after fraction addition when the specific value matches. EQUIVALENCE_CHANGED is a generic inequivalence explanation, not a confident pedagogical diagnosis. Classification never replaces the exact equivalence decision. Broader labels require labeled examples and false-positive measurement.
