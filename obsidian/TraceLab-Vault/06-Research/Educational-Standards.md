# Educational Standards

Use published Common Core mathematics as an alignment reference, not an endorsement. Fraction work maps broadly to grade 5 number-and-operations fractions; distribution and equivalent expressions to grade 6 expressions/equations; more advanced linear equations to grades 7–8. Exact per-template alignment and educator review remain pending. Primary document: https://www.thecorestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/Math_Standards.pdf .
