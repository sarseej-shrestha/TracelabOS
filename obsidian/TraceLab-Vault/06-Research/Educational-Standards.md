# Educational Standards

Use published Common Core mathematics as an alignment reference, not an endorsement. Fraction work maps broadly to grade 5 number-and-operations fractions; distribution and equivalent expressions to grade 6 expressions/equations; more advanced linear equations to grades 7–8. Exact per-template alignment and educator review remain pending. Primary document: https://www.thecorestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/Math_Standards.pdf .

## Verified fraction alignment references — 2026-10-09

Reviewed the official Common Core Mathematics PDF: grade 4 equivalence (4.NF.A.1) is prerequisite material; grade 5 fraction addition/subtraction including mixed numbers (5.NF.A.1) and multiplication/scaling (5.NF.B.4–5) inform the corresponding templates; general fraction division maps to 6.NS.A.1. Relevant printed pages: 30, 36–37 and 42. Source: [official mathematics standards](https://www.thecorestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/Math_Standards.pdf). This is developer-authored alignment, not endorsement or educator review. Full coverage of each referenced standard is not claimed.

Algebra alignment reviewed from the same official PDF: 5.OA.A.1 for grouped numerical operations; 6.EE.A.3–4 and 7.EE.A.1 for equivalent/collected expressions; 6.EE.B.7 and 7.EE.B.4a for supported equation forms; 8.EE.C.7b for distributing/collecting with variables on both sides (printed pages 44, 49 and 54). Negative-solution challenge items extend beyond the nonnegative scope of the grade-6 one-step reference. Exponents, multiple variables and degenerate equations are not included in these template claims.

Ratio alignment: 6.RP.A.2 and 6.RP.A.3a–c for rates, equivalent-ratio tables and percent part/whole; 7.RP.A.2b–c for proportional constants/equations; 7.RP.A.3 for percent changes. Checked against the [official standards PDF](https://www.thecorestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/Math_Standards.pdf), printed pages 42 and 48, on 2026-10-09. This is an implementation mapping, not coverage of every standard or an endorsement.
