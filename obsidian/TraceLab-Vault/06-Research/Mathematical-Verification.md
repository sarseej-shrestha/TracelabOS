# Mathematical Verification

Use exact rational affine normalization for a narrow, explainable domain. Domain restrictions must be checked before cancellation; identities and inconsistent equations are not automatically equivalent reasoning. SymPy is an independent reference, not an unquestionable ground truth. The offline oracle builds symbolic expressions from generated numeric parameters and never evaluates arbitrary student strings. Source: https://docs.sympy.org/latest/explanation/gotchas.html .
