# Privacy Requirements

The public-demo design is limited to fictional records and adult visitors. Do not solicit children’s information or infer FERPA/COPPA compliance from authentication. Photos can contain identifying metadata; local upload normalization removes metadata, but content itself can still identify people. Retention/deletion and institutional/legal review are prerequisites for any school deployment. No legal compliance assessment has been performed.
